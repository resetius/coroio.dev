Detailed Description

Delivery of TThreadedInbound to an actor system on another thread.

  • I/O side — Deliver pushes a batch into a TMpscQueue and wakes the actor side through a notifier of the actor system's poller if the queue was empty. Any number of I/O threads may deliver.
  • Actor side — a coroutine on that poller pops the batches and calls TActorSystem::Send for every envelope, in the order of Deliver calls of each thread. After every 2 MiB it lets the loop run its other coroutines and the actors, as the connections of TActorSystem::Serve(TSocket) do.

Full() is true while the bytes handed over and not passed to Send yet reach limit. A connection that it stops looks again only after 1 ms, so limit must hold more than a millisecond of the actor side's work: under backpressure the delivery passes at most about limit per millisecond (16 GB/s with the default, 1 GB/s with 1 MiB).

Note
Construct and destroy it on the thread of the actor system, before its loop runs and after the I/O threads that deliver to it have stopped. The destructor deletes the batches that were not delivered. The actor system outlives it.
Not available on Windows: it needs TNotifier.
